Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This is a personal, non-commercial application operated by one individual for their own household. It is not offered to the public, has no customers, and has no users other than the members of that household who have each granted access from their own Google Account.

What it accesses

With explicit Google account consent, and only for the accounts that granted it:

  • Gmail messages and settings — to read, search, label and organise mail, and to prepare drafts
  • Google Calendar — to read and maintain the household calendar
  • Google Docs and Google Sheets — to read and edit the household's own documents
  • Contacts — read only, to resolve names on messages and calendar entries

Why

To summarise and organise the household's own mail, keep a shared family calendar, and prepare drafts for a person to review before anything is sent.

Where the data goes

Data is processed on a private machine on the operator's home network. It is not sold, rented, published, or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit assessment, or to train any machine-learning model. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party data sharing of any kind.

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention

Only what is needed for the task at hand is retained, and it is held locally. Stored credentials are encrypted at rest. Data is deleted when it is no longer needed, and all stored data can be deleted on request by the account holder.

Revoking access

Any account holder may revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access stops immediately and no further data is retrieved.

Children

Where a minor in the household has granted access from their own account, that access is limited to that account and is managed by a parent. No information is shared outside the household.
